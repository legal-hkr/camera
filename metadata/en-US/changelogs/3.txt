v0.3.0

New:
- Pro mode: manual ISO and shutter speed up to 16 s (or one of them metered), manual focus with focus peaking, white balance in kelvins and a histogram
- Long exposures with a countdown on the shutter and crosshairs to help you hold still
- RAW (DNG) in Pro: alongside the photo from the lens in use, or a 50 MP DNG

Changes:
- All modes are now in the mode row, which follows your finger
- Buttons over the viewfinder no longer have a background; active settings are shown in amber
